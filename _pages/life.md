Outside my academic work, I enjoy backpacking, walking, reading, and all kinds of sports. I also love spending time outdoors and taking a break on a bench in a random park.

<p><strong>Backpacking &amp; Traveling</strong></p>

<p>
  I went on a solo trip to Europe with a giant backpack for 100 days, using all
  the money I had earned during my military service. I explored 11 countries and
  more than 20 cities, including Türkiye, Bulgaria, Romania, Hungary, Austria,
  Germany, Switzerland, Italy, Spain, France, and the United Kingdom.
</p>
