Research Experience

Student Directed Research Project, Ray Tracing Framework for Schwarzschild and Kerr Metrics:

Seoul National University

Jun. 2026–present

Advisor: Sascha Trippe

  • Secured approximately $2,000 in funding for a project I proposed.
  • Implemented a parallelized C++ Hamiltonian solver for photon geodesics in Kerr spacetime.
  • Extended the solver to ray trace thin Keplerian disks and generate relativistic images.
  • Developed a hands-on teaching tool to explore how light bending, Doppler shifts, and gravitational redshift shape disk images.
Self-Initiated Research Project, Multiple Massive Black Holes in the LUMINA Cosmological Simulation:

Center for Astrophysics | Harvard & Smithsonian and MIT

Feb. 2026–present

Advisors: Oliver Zier, LUMINA PI; Jacob Shen

  • Connected LUMINA cosmological merger histories with Holodeck binary evolution models to extend dual AGN forecasts to z = 6.6 and assess LISA detectability.
  • Translated these forecasts into survey requirements, estimating that a survey of approximately 300 arcmin² at 3 < z ≲ 6 could detect at least 10 potential dual AGNs with greater than 90% probability.
  • Studied the potential importance of MBH triplets for SMBH growth and demographics, estimating that approximately 20% of galaxies undergoing multiple mergers may temporarily host one.
Research Assistant, JWST Observability of SMBHs Across Seeding and Growth Scenarios:

University of Texas at Austin

Feb. 2025–Feb. 2026

Advisor: Volker Bromm

  • Integrated SMBH seeding and growth prescriptions to evaluate their photometric detectability with JWST.
  • Evaluated high-ionization line diagnostics using mock spectra where Balmer lines are inaccessible.
  • Projected that JWST could detect and identify SMBHs out to z ∼ 16 under optimistic assumptions.
Undergraduate Research Intern, AGN Activity and Star Formation: SED Analysis of Type 1 AGNs:

Seoul National University

Dec. 2023–Dec. 2024

Advisor: Jong-Hak Woo

  • Estimated SFRs of Type 1 AGN host galaxies by fitting their multiwavelength SEDs with CIGALE, using SDSS spectra and catalog photometry.
  • Tested how missing FIR data affected SFR estimates by comparing fits with and without FIR photometry, extending the analysis to hosts without FIR measurements.
  • Found a weak positive correlation between AGN activity and host galaxy SFR, with no evidence of SFR suppression in the sample.
