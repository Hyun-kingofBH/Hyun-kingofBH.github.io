Research Experience

Student Directed Research Project, Ray Tracing Framework for Schwarzschild and Kerr Metrics: Seoul National University | Jun. 2026–present. Secured approximately $2,000 in funding for a project I proposed; implemented a parallelized C++ Hamiltonian solver for photon geodesics in Kerr spacetime; extended the solver to ray trace thin Keplerian disks and generate relativistic images; developed a hands-on teaching tool to explore how light bending, Doppler shifts, and gravitational redshift shape disk images. Advisor: Sascha Trippe.

Research Assistant, JWST Observability of SMBHs Across Seeding and Growth Scenarios: University of Texas at Austin | Feb. 2025–Feb. 2026. Integrated SMBH seeding and growth prescriptions to evaluate their photometric detectability with JWST; evaluated high-ionization line diagnostics using mock spectra where Balmer lines are inaccessible; projected that JWST could detect and identify SMBHs out to $z\sim16$ under optimistic assumptions. Advisor: Volker Bromm.
Undergraduate Research Intern, AGN Activity and Star Formation: SED Analysis of Type 1 AGNs: Seoul National University | Dec. 2023–Dec. 2024. Estimated SFRs of Type 1 AGN host galaxies by fitting their multiwavelength SEDs with CIGALE, using SDSS spectra and catalog photometry; tested how missing FIR data affected SFR estimates by comparing fits with and without FIR photometry, extending the analysis to hosts without FIR measurements; found a weak positive correlation between AGN activity and host galaxy SFR, with no evidence of SFR suppression in the sample. Advisor: Jong-Hak Woo.
